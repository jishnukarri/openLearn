# openLearn: presenter script

Matches the 5 slides in `/admin/presentation`. Short on purpose: say it in your own words. Plain language, because the audience is new to AI. Anything in [square brackets] is yours to fill in.

**Three parts:** 1) the problem, 2) how AI fixes it, 3) how it works (the video), then the teacher demo.

## Slide 1: An AI tutor that never gets tired (0:08)

**Say:** "Hi, I'm [your name]. This is openLearn, an AI tutor that never gets tired. I'll show you the problem, how AI fixes it, and then it working."

## Slide 2: The problem: people forget (0:27)

**Say:** "People forget. That's normal. You understand a lesson, and weeks later some of it has gone. Sometimes a quick recap is enough. Often it isn't, and you need the whole lesson again. But a teacher can't re-teach every small topic for every student. There isn't the time. That's the gap."

*(Rubric: project clarity, purpose and audience.)*

---

## Slide 3: The fix: an AI that teaches it again (0:30)

**Say (keep it simple, this is where you show you understand AI):** "Here's how AI fixes it. The AI is a program that has learned from huge amounts of writing, so it can write explanations. I give it the topic and the exam board's rules. It writes a lesson, as many times as a student needs. It teaches the way a teacher does: it shows you, then does it with you, then you try. It answers questions. And when a student writes an answer, it checks it against the mark scheme."

**One line to land:** "It's not magic. It's a very fast writer that I point at one topic and one mark scheme."

*(Rubric: understanding of AI, creativity and originality.)*

**Click** to the video.

## Slide 5: Built for schools to run + teacher demo (0:22, then demo)

**Say:** "Does it replace the teacher? No. The teacher teaches the first lesson, and openLearn helps students go over it again. Teachers stay in charge. Let me show you what a teacher sees."

**Teacher demo (about 40 seconds, in your own words):** show the progress dashboard: who has done what, who is struggling, which topics are weak. Say what it lets a teacher do, for example "[the teacher sees at a glance who needs help, without marking anything]".

**Close:** "Schools use their own AI connection, so they control the cost. If you'd like to try it, scan the code. Thank you. Any questions?"

*(Rubric: practical use and impact, presentation skills.)*
