## Slide 2: The problem: people forget (0:40)

**Part 1 of 3: the problem.** Who has it: students, and the teachers who teach them.

**On screen:** Students forget / A recap isn't always enough / Teachers run out of time.

**Say:**
"Let's start with the problem. People forget. That's normal. You understand a lesson, and a few weeks later some of it has gone. Sometimes a quick recap brings it back. But often it doesn't. You need the whole lesson again, because there's one small detail you're missing, and until you get it, it just doesn't click. Some teachers do re-teach it. But a teacher can't do that for every small topic, for every student. There simply isn't the curriculum time. That's the gap."

**Optional personal line:** "[That's me too. I'd learn something, remember it for a long time, and then a detail would slip.]"

**Click.**

## Slide 3: The fix: an AI that teaches it again (0:45)

**Part 2 of 3: how AI fixes it.**

**On screen:** It builds the lesson / It teaches like a teacher / It answers and marks.

**Say:**
"Here's how I fix it with AI. First, the AI builds the lesson. Any topic, as many times as a student needs, without using up any class time. Second, it teaches the way a teacher does. It shows you, then it does it with you, then it lets you try. That's called I do, we do, you do. And third, it's always there to help. A student can ask the AI tutor a question and get a straight answer, and when they write an answer, it's marked point by point. It's a tutor that never gets tired. Let me show you how it works."

> The "straight answer" line describes the reworked tutor. Check it's true in the live site before you present.

**Click** to the video.

## Slide 4: Demo video (2:17)

**Part 3 of 3: how the platform does it.** The video is one continuous screen recording of a new student (called Mikey in the video) going from the website to a finished practice answer. It plays full screen with captions, a progress bar along the top, and a soft sound bed. Where a "3x speed" or "14x speed" badge shows, the footage is sped up (waiting and long typing). Say a line at each cue and let the rest breathe. Times are *from the start of the video*.

| 0:00 | New tab, address typed | "This is what a student does. They open the website." (let it play) |

| ~1:02 **I do** | Idea, then animated worked example | "First, *I do*. The platform shows the idea with an animated example, just like a teacher at the board." |

> This lesson has an *I do* step (animated example) and a *you do* step (question). It has no sliders, so don't say "they move the sliders". If you want to mention *we do*, say it's the short questions and activities in between, where the student works through it with the platform.

**Line to land (the teaching method):** "I do, we do, you do is a method schools already use, so this fits how teachers already teach."

**Pace tip:** you only need one sentence per cue. The silence between cues is fine: the captions and the screen are doing the work.
