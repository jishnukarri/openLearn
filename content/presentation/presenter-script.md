# openLearn: presenter script

Matches the 8 slides in `/admin/presentation`, in the same order. Edit freely. Anything in [square brackets] is for you to fill in or change.

**Realistic length: about 4 minutes 45 seconds** at a normal speaking pace (about 150 words a minute), including the 83-second video. The limit is 5 minutes, so you only have about 15 seconds of spare room. If you add anything, cut something else. Easy cuts: the optional personal line on slide 2, the extra sentence about photo upload on slide 5, and the last line of slide 7.

## Before you start

- Open `/admin/presentation` signed in as admin. Press **F** (or F5 on most clickers) for full screen. Press **T** to start the timer, which hides in full screen but keeps running.
- Clicker: PageDown / Right arrow = next slide, PageUp / Left arrow = previous. **B** or **.** blanks the screen if you want the room's attention.
- The demo video starts by itself on slide 4. Space pauses and plays it.
- Print the handout (`handout.pdf`) and put it on the tables or hand it out at the end.
- If the video won't play: say "It's a short walkthrough, so I'll describe it" and use the talking points under slide 4.

## Slide 1: AI that marks like a real teacher (0:18)

**On screen:** the openLearn logo, "AI that marks like a real teacher".

**Say:**
"Hi everyone, I'm [your name]. Today I'm going to show you openLearn, a revision platform I built to help students learn and revise better. In the next few minutes: the problem, why I built it, a short demo, and where it goes next."

**Click** when you finish the sentence.

---

## Slide 2: Learning it once isn't always enough (0:28)

**On screen:** three points: Everyone forgets / A recap isn't always enough / One missing detail.

**Say:**
"Let's start with the problem. Everyone forgets things, even after a lesson they understood well. Usually that's fine: you do a quick recap and it comes back. But sometimes a recap just isn't enough. You need the whole lesson again, because there's one specific detail you're missing, and until you find it, it doesn't click."

**Optional personal line:** "[That was me. I'd learn something, remember it for a long time, and then a detail would slip, and a summary didn't bring it back.]"

---

## Slide 3: So I built openLearn (0:38)

**On screen:** four steps: Find the gaps / Learn it properly / Check it stuck / Revise or redo. Line at the bottom: "Made because I loved the idea of helping students revise better."

**Say:**
"So I built openLearn. It works in four steps. First, a short quiz finds your gaps, so you skip what you already know. Then you learn the topic properly, step by step, with worked examples. Then you check it stuck with practice questions that are marked with feedback. And whenever you need to, you can revise quickly, or redo the whole lesson. I built it because I loved the idea of helping students revise better."

**Then:** "Let me show you what that looks like." Click to the video.

## Slide 6: Why it helps (0:26)

**On screen:** Revise or relearn / Skip what you know / Supports the teacher.

**Say:**
"Why does it help? First, students can revise quickly or relearn the whole lesson, whichever they need. Second, the starting quiz means nobody wastes time relearning what they already know. And third, it never replaces the teacher. The teacher teaches the first lesson, and openLearn supports the student with everything after."

## Slide 7: Built for schools to run (0:38)

**On screen:** We provide / Schools provide / Teachers stay in charge / Open by design.

**Say:**
"This is built for schools to run. I provide the platform: the lessons, quizzes, practice, flashcards and progress tracking. A school or teacher who wants to use it brings their own AI connection, so they stay in control of usage and cost. Teachers stay in charge. They teach the first lesson, and students use openLearn to revise and practise. And it's open by design, so any school or teacher can set it up for their own students."

> Keep this honest: say what's true for you. If you later offer hosting or pricing, change this slide and the handout first.

## Likely questions (prepare your answers)

- **"Does it replace the teacher?"** No. The teacher teaches the first lesson; openLearn supports revision and practice afterwards.
- **"Who pays for the AI?"** The school or teacher who runs it provides their own AI connection, so they control usage and cost. [Add anything you've decided about pricing.]
- **"How accurate is the marking?"** [Your honest answer. For example: "Answers are marked against the mark scheme and shown point by point, and a next step is comparing it with real teachers' marking."]
- **"Which exam boards?"** Currently Edexcel Computer Science and AQA Physics and Chemistry.
- **"Is it safe for students?"** [Your answer. The platform has an account system, teacher and admin roles, and a blocklist.]
- **"How did you build it?"** [Your own words. For example: "I wanted to solve my own problem as a student, so I just started building it."]
