# openLearn: my speech

## 1. Introduction

Hello everyone. I'm Jishnu from Year 11, and I'm presenting my platform, **openLearn**.

openLearn is a revision platform that gives students **bite-sized lessons** to help them relearn and understand topics they have forgotten.

## 2. The problem

I built it because of my own memory. I realised that some topics I learnt in Year 9 or Year 10 were the ones I forgot, or never fully understood. So what did I do? I went to my teachers and asked them to re-explain the topic, almost like a whole lesson again.

But a teacher can't do that for every topic. And every student is different: each one knows some things and is missing others.

## 3. The solution

So I built openLearn. It doesn't just help students revise a topic. It also gives them a **short lesson** on it, to fix any misunderstanding they have.

- It **teaches** the topic quickly.
- It **tests** them on it.
- It gives them **extra resources**, like flashcards, to take their revision further.

It is also very specific. It only uses the resources provided for each topic: the **official specification**, **revision notes**, and **exam questions** written by teachers or by the exam board itself.

## 5. Demo: the lesson

`1:08` The lessons work very similarly to how we learn at Fulwood. Each lesson focuses on a set of key points from the specification. It **teaches** each point, then **checks** that you understand it.

Here's an example, **Radioactive Decay**:

1. **The content.** First, the student reads the main idea.
2. `1:17` **I Do.** Then it shows a visual example that breaks it down step by step.
3. `1:36` **We Do.** Then the student is checked, with multiple choice or fill in the blanks, just like in class.

This repeats for every key point in the lesson. In this demo, those points are **alpha, beta and gamma radiation**.

## 6. Demo: extra practice

`1:55` Because this is revision, the student has learnt the topic before. So openLearn gives them extra practice in two ways: **exam questions** and **flashcards**.

`2:07` **Exam questions**

- The questions come from **past papers** or **teacher-made resources**.
- They are **reworded every time**, so students learn the idea, not the answer.
- Each question is linked to its **assessment objectives** and the part of the **specification** it comes from, so the marking is precise.
- If a student is stuck, they can ask for a **hint**.
- When they submit, they get **instant feedback**: what they did well and what they can improve.

`2:37` **Teachers stay in the loop**

This data is also shared with the teacher. It shows what students are generally struggling with, so the teacher can help them. That way, the teacher is always in the loop, and students get the best support.

`2:58` **Flashcards**

openLearn also has flashcard decks to help students remember content for the long term. Flashcards are **added automatically** as the student works through lessons.

## 7. How schools run it

openLearn isn't meant to be a separate company. It's **open-source software** that schools host themselves. The school covers the AI costs needed to run it. That means:

- Students use it **for free**.
- Internal staff documents **stay inside the school**, because the school chooses its own AI provider under its own agreement.
